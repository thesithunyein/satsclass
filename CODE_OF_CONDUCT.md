# Code of Conduct

SatsClass exists to help people learn, many of them for the first time. We want this project to feel the same way a good classroom does: patient, respectful and safe to ask questions in.

## Our standards

**Do:**

- Be welcoming to beginners. Every expert was once new.
- Assume good intent and ask before judging.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect different languages, countries and levels of English.
- Credit other people's ideas and code.

**Don't:**

- Harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately.
- Make discriminatory jokes or comments about identity, background or ability.
- Share someone's private information without their permission.
- Promote scams, tokens or "investment opportunities". This is a learning project.
- Continue a behavior after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, [@thesithunyein](https://github.com/thesithunyein), through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/thesithunyein/satsclass/security/advisories/new) (choose it even for conduct reports; it stays private). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, the maintainer may:

1. Send a private warning.
2. Remove the offending content.
3. Temporarily ban the person from the project.
4. Permanently ban the person from the project.
